ABSTRACT
       This project aims to develop an intelligent tax management assistant system for Live Streamers, a profession characterized by irregular income and a large number of daily transactions. Recording income and calculating annual taxes can be particularly challenging for Live Streamers. The system is designed to help users conveniently, accurately, and systematically record their income from multiple platforms. It also links income details with Thai tax regulations based on Section 40(8) of the Revenue Code to automatically assess income according to the progressive tax rate structure.
       The tax assistant application for Streamers consists of key modules, including a multi-entry daily income recording system, monthly and annual income summary modules, an automated tax calculation system using standard expense deductions, and a tax summary interface that clearly displays assessable income, net taxable income, and the amount of tax due. The system emphasizes ease of use, enabling users to evaluate their taxes without requiring prior tax knowledge.
       For development, web application technologies were used: React for the user interface and Node.js with Express.js for data processing and management. A MySQL database stores income and expense records, protected through data encryption and JWT token authentication to ensure data security.
       The outcome of this project is expected to help Live Streamers efficiently manage their income and calculate taxes, reducing the burden of manual keeping and increasing their understanding of their tax obligations. The system aims to become a practical tax assistant that simplifies tax planning and annual tax filing for Live Streamers.
Keywords: tax, tax application, tax calculation, income and expense management
